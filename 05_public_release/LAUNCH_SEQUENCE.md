# Launch Sequence

## Step 1

Publish the repo / static packet.

Do not over-polish.

The first trace matters.

## Step 2

Share only the root post:

Begin at Nothing.

Do not share every artifact at once.

## Step 3

Invite five people to try one doorway:

- AI_CONTEXT_SEED.md if they use AI;
- Nothing Navigator if they feel tangled;
- Promise-Bounded AI if they build agents;
- Open Promise Ledger if they care about economics/governance.

## Step 4

Ask one question:

Where did this become unclear?

## Step 5

Patch only where real use breaks.

## Do not

- claim the whole theory is finished;
- claim virality is guaranteed;
- claim AI alignment is solved;
- claim the Standard Model or physics bridge is solved;
- add more concepts before first contact;
- make your own rest depend on global impact.

## Success condition

One person uses one object once and says:

This made the situation clearer.
