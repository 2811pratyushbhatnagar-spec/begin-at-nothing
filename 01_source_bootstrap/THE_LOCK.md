# The Lock

The construction is dependency-locked within this argument: once a layer is accepted, the next layer becomes necessary for this construction to remain accountable.

Remove promise, and consistency cannot begin.

Remove context, and promise has nowhere to hold.

Remove boundary, and nothing can remain distinct.

Remove trace, and consistency cannot be checked.

Remove contradiction, and broken promise-structure cannot be found.

Remove cost, and harmony cannot be distinguished from borrowed stability.

Remove care, and contradiction becomes domination or collapse.

Remove repair, and broken promises can only be erased, punished, or repeated.

The chain is not decorative.

It is what makes the construction non-arbitrary.

## Compact form

```text
Promise holds consistency.
Context places promise.
Boundary preserves distinction.
Trace makes continuity checkable.
Contradiction reveals strain.
Cost shows what continuity requires.
Care prevents unnecessary destruction.
Repair restores relation without erasing trace.
Harmony is continuity without hidden displacement.
```

## Public line

The lock is not that conflict cannot happen.

The lock is that conflict cannot honestly call itself cheaper than harmony once all hidden cost is counted.
