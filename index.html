How to read this packet

This packet makes one core construction: coherent representation requires something to hold. It calls that holding “promise-like.”

Read “promise” first as a minimal condition of consistency — not as spirituality, morality, or a completed theory.

The AI, ledger, economics, and governance sections are design extensions of that construction. They are not proofs that these domains are solved.

Registers used here: exact / inspectable artifact, structural design, open research direction, and explicit nonclaim.
