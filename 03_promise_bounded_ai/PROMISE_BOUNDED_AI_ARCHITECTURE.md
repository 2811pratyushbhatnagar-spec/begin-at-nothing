# Promise-Bounded AI Architecture v0.1

**An open design for agents that act through promises, boundaries, traces, costs, and repair.**

AI agents do not just need orchestration.

They need promises.

## Kernel

Agents should act through:

- explicit promises;
- scoped boundaries;
- visible traces;
- bounded costs;
- repairable outcomes.

## Six core objects

### Context

How the situation is placed before AI acts.

### Promise

What the agent is authorized to pursue.

### Boundary

What it may access, affect, reveal, store, change, or escalate.

### Trace

What it did, why it did it, and what remains.

### Cost

Compute, money, time, attention, privacy, labor, energy, ecology, social cost, emotional cost, trust cost, and future repair.

### Repair

What happens if the action fails, harms, exceeds scope, or becomes unclear.

## Minimum execution gate

No agent action should execute unless it has:

1. authorized promise;
2. scoped boundary;
3. cost budget;
4. trace requirement;
5. repair path;
6. human review when action affects another person, money, privacy, health, legal status, reputation, safety, or irreversible systems.

## v0.1 boundary

v0.1 is not an autonomous agent system.

It is a static, inspectable, local-first architecture for shaping, bounding, validating, and repairing AI action before execution.

It should not include accounts, tracking, analytics, hidden telemetry, storage by default, autonomous execution, or external tool use without review.

## Anti-capture rules

No person is reducible to a ledger.

No agent acts without a promise.

No promise acts without a boundary.

No boundary is crossed without consent or lawful authority.

No action is complete without a trace.

No cost may be hidden.

No failure is acceptable without a repair path.

No global layer may consume private life.

No AI becomes judge, priest, owner, state, or whole.
