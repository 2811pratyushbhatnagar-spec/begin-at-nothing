# Promise-Bounded AI Architecture v0.1

**An open design for agents that act through promises, boundaries, traces, costs, and repair.**

AI agents do not just need orchestration.

They need promises.

## What this is / What this is not

### What this is

Promise-Bounded AI is a small design seed for making AI actions easier to inspect, limit, and repair.

Its first promise is simple:

Before an AI system acts, it should make clear:

- what promise it is keeping;
- what boundary it is respecting;
- what trace it leaves;
- what cost it may create;
- and how repair remains possible.

Decisions remain with the human.

The agent's promise should be visible at each step.

This is a context and governance layer for human-AI interaction. It helps shape action before execution.

### What this is not

It is not a complete alignment solution.

It is not a governance system.

It is not autonomous infrastructure.

It does not track people.

It does not store accounts.

It does not make decisions for users.

It is not a replacement for consent, judgment, or repair.

It does not solve the full technical AI alignment problem, including reward hacking, deceptive alignment, scalable oversight, corrigibility, interpretability, or mesa-optimization.

### First rule

No AI action without:

- authorized promise;
- scoped boundary;
- visible trace;
- bounded cost;
- repair path;
- and human review when action affects another person, money, privacy, health, legal status, reputation, safety, or irreversible systems.

## Kernel

Agents should act through:

- explicit promises;
- scoped boundaries;
- visible traces;
- bounded costs;
- repairable outcomes.

## Six core objects

### Context

How the situation is placed before AI acts.

### Promise

What the agent is authorized to pursue.

### Boundary

What it may access, affect, reveal, store, change, or escalate.

### Trace

What it did, why it did it, and what remains.

### Cost

Compute, money, time, attention, privacy, labor, energy, ecology, social cost, emotional cost, trust cost, and future repair.

### Repair

What happens if the action fails, harms, exceeds scope, or becomes unclear.

## Minimum execution gate

No agent action should execute unless it has:

1. authorized promise;
2. scoped boundary;
3. cost budget;
4. trace requirement;
5. repair path;
6. human review when action affects another person, money, privacy, health, legal status, reputation, safety, or irreversible systems.

## v0.1 boundary

v0.1 is not an autonomous agent system.

It is a static, inspectable, local-first architecture for shaping, bounding, validating, and repairing AI action before execution.

It should not include accounts, tracking, analytics, hidden telemetry, storage by default, autonomous execution, or external tool use without review.

## Anti-capture rules

No person is reducible to a ledger.

No agent acts without a promise.

No promise acts without a boundary.

No boundary is crossed without consent or lawful authority.

No action is complete without a trace.

No cost may be hidden.

No failure is acceptable without a repair path.

No global layer may consume private life.

No AI becomes judge, priest, owner, state, or whole.
