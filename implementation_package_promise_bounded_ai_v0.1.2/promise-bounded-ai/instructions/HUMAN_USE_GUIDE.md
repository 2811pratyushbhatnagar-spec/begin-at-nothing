# Human Use Guide

Use this package when a situation is messy and you want AI help without handing the whole situation to the AI in a distorted form.

Start with the Navigator.

Name one thing.

Do not make it the whole.

Let the Navigator help you find:

- the boundary,
- the promise,
- the trace,
- the contradiction,
- the cost,
- the care direction,
- the smallest truthful next move.

Then, if useful, paste the summary into an AI.

Use AI only to clarify, compress, translate, draft, or place.

Do not let AI become authority, diagnosis, doctrine, spiritual confirmation, or replacement for lived judgment.
