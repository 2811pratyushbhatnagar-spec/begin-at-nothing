# Anti-Capture Rules

No person is reducible to a ledger.

No agent acts without a promise.

No promise acts without a boundary.

No boundary is crossed without consent or lawful authority.

No action is complete without a trace.

No cost may be hidden.

No failure is acceptable without a repair path.

No global layer may consume private life.

No AI becomes judge, priest, owner, state, or whole.

No system may call itself aligned by hiding its cost inside another boundary.
