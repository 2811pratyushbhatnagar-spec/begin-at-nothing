Nothing Navigator — Static Version

Start from near-nothing.

Use this if the interactive Navigator does not render in your browser or file preview. Read slowly. Stop wherever contact becomes clear.

Safety first

If there is immediate danger, abuse, medical risk, self-harm risk, coercion, or threat of violence, protect life first. If outside the US, contact your local emergency number, crisis line, trusted person, or nearest emergency service.

0 — Nothing

Before the story, before the role, before the fear or the solution — what is here? Name one thing. Do not fill this too quickly.

1 — Something

You named one thing. Do not make it the whole. Can this be affected?

2 — Boundary

What could be harmed, protected, or lost here? A body. A relationship. A promise. A dignity. A livelihood. A trust. A future. A life. If a living boundary is at risk, protect it first.

3 — Promise

What was expected to hold? A promise may be spoken or unspoken. A promise is a thread through time.

4 — Trace

What has happened? What remains? Do not erase the trace. Do not worship the trace. Let it show what it shows. No more. No less.

5 — Contradiction

What cannot currently coexist? Two needs. Two promises. Two identities. Two fears. Two truths. Two ways of staying safe. Two futures.

6 — Emotion / Cost

Where is the cost being felt? Emotion is not noise. Emotion is also not the whole truth. Emotion shows that something matters.

7 — Care / Repair

What can be preserved without lying? Care is intelligence refusing unnecessary destruction. If repair is possible, begin repair. If repair is not possible yet, protect the boundary. If the situation is unsafe, step back or seek help.

8 — Action

What is the smallest truthful next move? One sentence. One pause. One boundary. One question. One refusal. One apology. One clarification. One act of care. One request for help. One record.

9 — Return

After the move, what remains? If things are clearer, let clarity settle. If distortion increased, repair or revise. If a new trace appears, begin again. If nothing more is needed, stop.

Final line: Do not force closure. Read what remains. Place it clearly. Act where contact becomes possible.
