Nothing Navigator

Start from near-nothing.

Read slowly. Stop wherever contact becomes clear.

Safety first

If there is immediate danger, abuse, medical risk, self-harm risk, coercion, or threat of violence, protect life and seek real-world help first.

0 — Nothing

Before the story, before the role, before the fear or the solution — what is here?

1 — Something

Name one thing. Only one. Let it be small. Do not make it the whole.

2 — Boundary

What can be affected? What must not be casually crossed?

3 — Promise

What was expected to hold? A promise is a thread through time.

4 — Trace

What has happened? What remains? Do not erase the trace. Do not make it the whole.

5 — Contradiction

What cannot currently coexist?

6 — Emotion / Cost

Where is the cost being felt? Emotion is not noise. Emotion is also not the whole truth.

7 — Care / Repair

What can be preserved without lying? Care is intelligence refusing unnecessary destruction.

8 — Action

What is the smallest truthful next move?

9 — Return

After the move, what remains? If nothing more is needed, stop.

Do not force closure. Read what remains. Place it clearly. Act where contact becomes possible.
