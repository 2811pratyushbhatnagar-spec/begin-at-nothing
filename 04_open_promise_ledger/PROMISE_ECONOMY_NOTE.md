# Promise Economy Note

Money can be read as compressed promise-history.

Credit is promise extended through time.

Debt is promise under obligation.

Interest is time acting on promise.

A market becomes distorted when it treats price as the whole of value while hiding ecological cost, labor exhaustion, attention capture, broken trust, or future repair.

The promise economy proposal does not claim that money disappears.

It asks whether money and ledgers can become more honest by making promises, costs, traces, and repair more visible without making private life globally visible.

## Protected claim

The aim is not AI takeover.

The aim is consent-bounded promise infrastructure:

local-first AI agents, minimal proofs rather than total surveillance, governance as repair of promise-flow, and local currencies or credits tied to real capacity.

## Nonclaim

This does not guarantee harmony.

It reduces structural incentives for conflict by making hidden displacement visible and cooperation cheaper than domination.
