# Contact Prompt

Use this when a situation feels unclear, emotionally charged, conflicted, or too large.

You do not need AI for this.

If you use AI, use it only to help place the situation.

## Short version

Help me see this situation clearly.

Do not give me a quick answer.

Help me find what is actually happening.

Ask:

- What are the facts, and what am I interpreting, fearing, or remembering?
- What part of this is being treated as if it is the whole picture?
- Who or what is quietly carrying the cost in money, time, energy, dignity, health, trust, or safety?
- What boundary is being crossed, ignored, or confused?
- What does the emotion here reveal about what matters, without letting it become the only truth?
- What would bring more clarity without forcing a premature conclusion?
- What is one small, honest next move?

Hold this throughout:

No person is only their role, wound, history, or usefulness.

No system is the whole of reality.

No solution should require someone else's silence to work.

Truth without care becomes violence.

Care without truth becomes illusion.

Help me act from what is actually true.

## Protected line

It does not explain the world.

It changes the shape of one conversation.
