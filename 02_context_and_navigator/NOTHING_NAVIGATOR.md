# Nothing Navigator

A standalone sequence for returning from confusion to contact.

No platform required. No belief required. No special language required.

Read slowly. Stop wherever contact becomes clear.

## Safety first

If there is immediate danger, abuse, medical risk, self-harm risk, coercion, or threat of violence, protect life and seek real-world help first.

Return to this only when the boundary is stable enough.

This is not therapy and not a substitute for professional care.

## 0. Nothing

Before the name, role, story, wound, solution, demand, fear, or identity:

What is here?

If nothing clear appears, let that be true.

Nothing does not need to be filled too quickly.

## 1. Something

Name one thing that is actually present.

Only one.

Let it be small.

Do not make it the whole.

## 2. Boundary

What can be affected?

Where is the edge?

What must not be casually crossed?

If a living boundary is at risk, protect it first.

## 3. Promise

What is being relied on?

What was expected to hold?

A promise is a thread through time.

## 4. Trace

What has happened?

What remains?

Do not erase the trace.
Do not worship the trace.

Let it show what it shows.

## 5. Contradiction

What cannot currently coexist?

What is being forced together too soon?

What is being kept apart too harshly?

## 6. Emotion / Cost

Where is the cost being felt?

Emotion is not noise.

Emotion is also not the whole truth.

Emotion shows that something matters.

## 7. Care / Repair

What can be preserved without lying?

What must change?

What cannot honestly continue?

Care is intelligence refusing unnecessary destruction.

## 8. Action

What is the smallest truthful next move?

One sentence. One pause. One boundary. One question. One refusal. One apology. One clarification. One act of care. One request for help. One record.

Do not force the whole future into one move.

## 9. Return

After the move, what remains?

If things are clearer, let clarity settle.

If distortion increased, repair or revise.

If a new trace appears, begin again.

If nothing more is needed, stop.

## Final line

Do not force closure.

Read what remains.

Place it clearly.

Act where contact becomes possible.
