# Register and Nonclaims

## Registers

- **Exact / proven** — use only for formal proof, executable verification, or directly inspectable implementation.
- **Structural / design-level** — architectures, mappings, and constrained analogies.
- **Open / exploratory** — promising research directions that are not solved.
- **Blocked / not yet solved** — where a necessary bridge or implementation is missing.
- **Withdrawn / corrected** — prior claims that should not be repeated as active claims.

## Nonclaims

This packet does not claim:

- that all physics is derived here;
- that the Standard Model bridge is solved;
- that AI alignment is solved;
- that money or governance can be fixed by one document;
- that harmony is guaranteed;
- that conflict disappears;
- that the framework is complete;
- that the map is the world;
- that people should obey AI;
- that a ledger can represent a whole person;
- that one artifact can replace care, law, science, politics, therapy, or lived judgment.

## Active claim

Any coherent representation requires something to hold.

That holding is promise-like.

Once affected boundaries enter the field, promise requires trace, cost, care, and repair.

## Public rule

Do not make one fragment carry the whole.
